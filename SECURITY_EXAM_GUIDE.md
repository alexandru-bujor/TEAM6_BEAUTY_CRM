# Secure Application Development – Exam Quick Guide

## 1) Main Exam: Performance Review
- **You must prepare (your personal work):**
  - 1–2 page Work Log (security-focused).
  - Evidence Folder (code, commits, diagrams, tests).
  - 1–10 Self-Evaluation.
- **What happens:**
  - Team Leader presents the app (2–3 min) and describes your contribution.
  - You show evidence and answer security questions.

## 2) Optional Grade-Upgrade Exam: Crisis Scenario
- Only for students who want to improve their grade.
- You receive a surprise security incident (SQL injection, brute-force, credential leak, etc.).
- **You have 3–5 minutes** to analyze, then a hot seat:
  - How does this impact your code?
  - How would you fix it?
  - How would you test your fix?
  - What risks remain?
- Strong answers = potential grade improvement. Weak answers do **not** lower the grade.
